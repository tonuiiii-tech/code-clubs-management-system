# Risk Register

**Project:** STEAMLabs Africa Code Clubs Management System
**Last updated:** 2026-10-21

Risk ratings are initial estimates and should be reviewed with the STEAMLabs team. Likelihood and impact use Low, Medium and High. Every risk below is **open**.

| ID    | Risk                                                                                                                   | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                  | Owner / review point                                |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |

## Review process

- Review high-impact risks at sprint planning and sprint review.
- Update the risk owner and mitigation as decisions are made.
- Add newly discovered risks rather than silently changing requirements.
- Do not mark a risk resolved until the mitigation is implemented and verified.
